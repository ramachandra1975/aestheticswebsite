Profile

The Mind Behind aestheticsofsociety

Naredla Ramachandra is a freelance writer, blogger and editor with 8+ years of experience working across diverse domains. A self-described "secret blogger," he has dedicated years to researching the philosophical depths of Indian classical arts — especially Natya Shastra and its cross-references to the Vedas, Upanishads and ancient cosmic sciences.

His research is not academic in the conventional sense — it is lived, intuitive and deeply personal. He believes that every step a dancer takes has a direct connection to the universe, and that music, dance and art are ultimately tools for spiritual realization and the resurrection of Sanatana Dharma.

Mission

What aestheticsofsociety Stands For

The aestheticsofsociety platform was created to bridge the gap between ancient Indian knowledge systems and the modern world. Through YouTube videos, WordPress articles and social media, Ramachandra aims to educate dancers, gurus, scholars and art lovers on the practical importance of Vedic texts, Sanskrit grammar and the Natya Shastra.

The blog's subtitle — Dharmo Rakshati Rakshitaha — captures the philosophy: when we protect Dharma, Dharma protects us. Art, music and culture are not separate from this protection — they are its very expression.

Research Areas

Topics of Deep Study

  • Natya Shastra by Bharata Muni — comprehensive cross-referencing
  • Maheshwara Sutras and Sanskrit language phonetics
  • Rasa Theory (Abhinava Gupta) & Dhwani Theory (Ananda Vardhana)
  • Chandogya Upanishad, Taittiriya Upanishad & Vedic cosmology
  • Vivekachudamani — Adi Shankaracharya's philosophy
  • Hiranya Garbha Sukta & its connection to classical dance
  • History of Indian aesthetics from 7th century to present
  • Yoga Shastra by Siddhendra Yogi & Kuchipudi dance tradition
  • Vishnu Sahasranama & Lalitha Sahasranama valli
